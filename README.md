# Jetpack Joyride Takeover

**Minecraft** × Jetpack Joyride · visual · idea by the team

Bring Jetpack Joyride into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> Strapped a machine-gun jetpack to Minecraft and flew through an endless zapper-filled lab, scaring scientists and grabbing coins.

## Install

Drop the mod jar and Fabric API in your Minecraft 26.3 Fabric mods folder, start a world, and you spawn wearing the jetpack. Hold Jump in the air to fly and shoot, and follow the lab hall east.
